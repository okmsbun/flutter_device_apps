# Code of conduct

Keep issues, pull requests, and discussions respectful and focused on the project.

- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, and sharing private information are not acceptable.
- Give constructive feedback and respect different experience levels.

For conduct concerns, mention @okmsbun in the relevant thread or use GitHub's
**Report content** action. The maintainer may remove disruptive content or restrict
participation.
